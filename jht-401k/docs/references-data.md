## Notes

```
Contains information from CPF contribution rates and CPF allocation rates accessed on 21 October 2026
from data.gov.sg (Central Provident Fund Board) which is made available under the terms of the
Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0 https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence
```

**Titles.**
- Titles are taken from the covers. Each cover also prints a theme line (for example "A Challenging Year" for 2006, or "Inclusive Coverage, Trusted Services" for 2025). The themes are left out, matching the style of `references-draft.md`.

**Open at the first check** (items 1 to 5 are resolved in the Changes sections below; item 6 stands).
1. The author name "PT Jamsostek (Persero)" will fail the shared gate's citation check (`gate/checks/cite.py`). I tested the gate's own functions. In the APA form "(PT Jamsostek, 2007)", the citation is read but matches no entry. In the nested form "(PT Jamsostek (Persero), 2007)", no citation is read at all, so every Jamsostek entry would be reported as never cited. The cause is the `\b` word boundary placed after an author name that ends in ")". With the author written "PT Jamsostek", both directions match. APA 7 (9.29) drops business-structure designations from publisher names, and "(Persero)" is one (it marks a state-owned limited company). That supports the shorter form, but the brief asked for the name as printed, so the entries keep "(Persero)" until the owner decides.
2. Order of 2016a and 2016b: they follow title order, as the brief asked. From memory, and not checked against the manual, APA 7 (9.47) puts a same-year reference with only a year before one with a full date. If that is right, the integrated report would be 2016a and the news release 2016b.
3. Two years rest on judgement rather than a printed date. For `bpjs_ir_2019.pdf` the entry gives 2020, but the PDF was created on 2 February 2021. For `bpjs_ir_2018.pdf` the entry gives 2019, but the PDF was created in March 2020.
4. The IMF entry has a single retrieval date (October 22, 2026, when the US file was fetched). The Indonesia file was retrieved on October 16, 2026, at Post 27's vet.
5. The task named only `cpf_contribution_rates.csv`, but asked for "the CPF Board datasets". Only the contribution rates feed a figure; `profile_data.py` also profiles the allocation rates. The allocation data set gets its own entry because the draft says "CPF Board datasets" and the required notice names both. Drop 2026a, and relabel 2026b as 2026, if the post stops naming it.
6. `data/ilo_idn_employment_by_status.csv` was not in the brief and has no entry here. The current draft does not cite the ILO.
