# Rights notes: Post 32

- American Time Use Survey files and the Employer Costs for Employee Compensation release: U.S. Bureau of Labor
  Statistics, works of the U.S. government. The post publishes only aggregates computed from the survey file; the
  file itself stays out of the repository (`data/README.md` says how to download it).
- Fact Sheets #23 and #17A and the regulation in 29 CFR part 541: U.S. Department of Labor and the Electronic Code
  of Federal Regulations, works of the U.S. government.
- Undang-Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2026: Indonesian legislation, from the official database of the Audit Board of
  Indonesia (JDIH BPK).
- Pencavel (2014), Shangguan, DeVaro and Owan (2021), Hafner et al. (2016) and Boushey and Glynn (2012): cited, with
  short quotes in `docs/sources.json` and `docs/provenance-audit.md`. Chart 2 is drawn from the curve the post
  computes from Pencavel's published estimates, not copied from his figures, and is credited in its footer.
- Stored copies of every cited document stay in `sources/`, which the repository ignores.
